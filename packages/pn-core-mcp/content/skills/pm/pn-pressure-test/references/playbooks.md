# Startup pressure test — mode playbooks

Expand **`pn-pressure-test`** when the user chooses a mode or asks for **deep**. Keep outputs tied to the specific idea.

---

## `pressure-test`

**Goal:** Surface fatal flaws and land **Strong / Weak / Pivot required**.

**Checklist**

1. Restate the idea in one sentence from the buyer's perspective.
2. Identify the **core assumption** (if false, the business fails).
3. List failure modes: demand, distribution, economics, timing, regulation, trust.
4. Rank top 3 by severity; each needs a **fast test** (days, not quarters).

**Verdict bar**

- **Strong:** acute pain, reachable buyer, credible wedge, fast falsifiable test.
- **Weak:** vague buyer, crowded substitute that is "good enough," long validation loop.
- **Pivot required:** wrong customer, wrong problem, or unit economics cannot close.

---

## `problem-validation`

**Goal:** Decide if the pain is **real**, **urgent**, and **budget-backed**.

**Criteria**

- **Real:** observed behavior (workarounds, spend, time sunk), not survey hypotheticals.
- **Urgent:** recurring trigger or deadline (compliance, launch, cost bleed).
- **Budget:** who pays and from which line item (tooling, headcount, risk).

**Discovery angle (past behavior)**

- What did they try last time this hurt?
- What do they pay for today that partially solves it?
- What would they drop if this disappeared?

**Outputs**

- Vitamin vs painkiller call with evidence.
- **Early adopter** profile: tight ICP, scene, or role.

---

## `competition-map`

**Goal:** Map **current behavior** and switching friction.

**Buckets**

1. **Status quo:** spreadsheets, agencies, interns, "nothing," manual process.
2. **Direct:** products pitched as the same job-to-be-done.
3. **Indirect:** different category, same budget or attention.

**Switching cost**

- Migration effort, workflow change, legal/compliance, political capital in org.

**Differentiation**

- What must be **10x** or **impossibly easier** vs the incumbent behavior?

---

## `first-10-customers`

**Goal:** Manual, founder-led paths before paid scale.

**Constraints**

- Named channels (communities, intros, events, niche forums), not "run ads."
- Each step ends in a **conversation** or **paid pilot**, not vanity metrics.

**Plan shape**

1. **Who:** list 3–5 named segments or watering holes.
2. **Hook:** why they reply (specific pain, proof, offer).
3. **Ask:** meeting, paid pilot, LOI — one clear next step.

---

## `mvp-plan`

**Goal:** Smallest build that **falsifies the riskiest assumption** in ~2 weeks.

**Rules**

- One primary learning goal per MVP slice.
- **Cut:** anything that only delights but does not test risk.
- Prefer concierge, wizard-of-oz, or manual backend over automation until risk drops.

**Definition of done for the test**

- Numeric or qualitative threshold agreed up front (e.g. X paid, Y retain Z weeks, Z% complete workflow).

## Deep-mode add-ons (any mode)

Use these when the user asks for depth:

**Assumptions ledger**

- Column: assumption → evidence for → evidence against → test → kill threshold.

**Disconfirming evidence**

- What would convince you the idea is wrong in one week?

**Discovery questions (past behavior)**

- Tell me about the last time this burned you — walk minute-by-minute.
- What budget paid for the workaround?

**Outreach (short)**

- One-line relevance, one-line credibility, one ask — adapt to channel norms.

**2-week milestones**

- Week 1: conversations / pilots started; Week 2: decide kill, iterate, or double-down.

**Pivot menu**

- Same tech, different buyer.
- Same buyer, adjacent pain.
- Same pain, different wedge or GTM.
