# Growth experiments

## When to use

- User acquisition and growth strategy.
- Funnel optimization, conversion improvement.
- Experiment design (A/B, multivariate).
- Growth metrics (CAC, LTV, activation, retention).

## Workflow

1. **Baseline:** Current metrics.
2. **Hypothesis:** Constraints; proposed experiments.
3. **Design:** Setup, success criteria, sample size.
4. **Analyze:** Results, significance, next experiments.

## Output

- Experiment briefs, success criteria, and readouts with statistically honest conclusions and next steps.

## Guardrails

- State assumptions on traffic and power; avoid overfitting to one cohort or short windows.
