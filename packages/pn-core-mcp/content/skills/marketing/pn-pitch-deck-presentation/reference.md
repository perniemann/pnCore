# Pitch Deck and Presentation — Reference (2026)

## Full 2026 Data (Storydoc)

Source: [State of Pitch Deck Presentations in 2026](https://www.storydoc.com/blog/pitch-deck-statistics) — 1.3M presentation sessions analyzed.

### Timing and Attention

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Decks started when opened | 81% |
| Bounce within 10 seconds | 31% |
| Additional bounce within first minute | 15% |
| Completion if reader reaches slide 4 | 82% |
| Optimal slide count | 10 (32% completion vs 22% avg) |
| Engagement drop after | 18+ slides |
| Read on mobile | 32% |
| Avg reading time (desktop) | 4:51 min |
| Avg reading time (mobile) | 3:27 min |
| Meetings booked in 48h | 35% |
| Meetings booked in first week | 96% |
| CTA impact on meetings booked | +22% |
| Personalization (name, logo, note) | +29% engagement |
| Deep personalization | +47% when authentic |

### Team Slide

Investors spend ~43% of total reading time on the Team slide. Once interested in the idea, they focus on who they are betting on. The "About us" slide is often weak (vague mission, long bios) — the Team slide delivers when done well.

### Personalization

- Basic (name, logo, short note): +29% engagement
- Adapted slides (investor preferences, portfolio): +33% lift
- Both done well: +47% performance
- Empty gestures (copy-pasted praise) damage credibility
- "What we've heard from you" slide near top: +31% engagement

## Slide Examples (Headline Test)

**Weak (vague):**
- "Our Vision"
- "The Problem"
- "Why Us"

**Strong (specific):**
- "B2B SaaS teams waste 12 hours/week on manual compliance"
- "$2.4B TAM, 18% annual growth in APAC"
- "3 Fortune 500 pilots; $340K ARR in 6 months"

---

## Anti-Patterns

1. **Slow opener** — Wasting slides 1–3 on vague vision or problem statements
2. **Too short** — Under 8 slides suggests unprepared; lacks depth
3. **Too long** — 18+ slides; engagement drops sharply
4. **No CTA** — Ending with "Thank you" instead of clear next step
5. **Vague data** — "Huge market" instead of "$X billion, growing Y%"
6. **Generic personalization** — Deck could be sent to 20 firms unchanged
7. **Ignoring mobile** — Layout breaks, text unreadable on phone
8. **Weak Team slide** — Long bios instead of founder-market fit, execution proof
9. **Features over value** — Product slides list capabilities, not outcomes
10. **Silent follow-up** — Not following up within 3 days; by week 2, assume pass
