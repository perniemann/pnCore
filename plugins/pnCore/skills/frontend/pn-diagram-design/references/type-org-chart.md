# Type: org-chart

Ownership and routing. Mermaid: `flowchart TB` (tree). Editorial: parent centered or top, children evenly spaced, orthogonal elbows.

## Grammar

- Nodes are **roles or teams**, not people unless names are the point. Edges are reports-to or routes-to.
- Depth ≤3. Width: split into overview + one team rather than 20 leaf boxes.
- Accent the node the reader must find (the owner of the decision).

## Cuts

Drop dotted “informs” lines unless they are the subject. Assistants and vacant seats are footnotes. If containment (not reporting) is the story, say nested and still use this type or layers — do not invent a second grammar.
