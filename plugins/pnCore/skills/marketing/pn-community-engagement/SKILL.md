# Community engagement

## When to use

- Community strategy (Reddit, Discord, forums, Slack).
- Authentic engagement and reputation building.
- AMA, Q&A, expert session planning.
- Brand mention monitoring and response.

## Workflow

1. **Research:** Identify communities; learn rules and culture.
2. **Participation:** Consistent helpful engagement; no promotion first.
3. **Content:** Educational posts, resources, problem-solving answers.
4. **Initiatives:** AMA, Q&A when ready; prepare for value delivery.

## Guardrails

- 90/10 rule: 90% value-add, 10% max promotional.
- Never spam; follow each community's rules.
- Build reputation over months.

## Integration

- **pn-copywriter** — Educational posts and responses.
