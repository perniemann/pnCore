# Pitch Deck and Presentation

## When to use

- Investor pitch deck or fundraising materials
- Investor update or board deck
- Sales deck or product pitch to prospects
- Internal or executive presentation
- Conference talk or high-stakes pitch
- User mentions pitch deck, presentation, slides, investor materials, fundraising deck

## 2026 Principles

Evidence from 1.3M+ presentation sessions (Storydoc, 2026):

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| 31% bounce within 10 seconds | First slide must convey clarity and momentum |
| 82% who reach slide 4 finish the deck | Front-load substance in slides 1–3 |
| 10 slides = 32% completion (vs 22% avg) | Target 10–15 slides; avoid 18+ |
| 32% read on mobile | Design for mobile readability |
| 35% of meetings booked in 48h, 96% in 7 days | Include clear CTA; follow up within 3 days |

**Core rules:**
- **Headline test**: Slide titles alone should explain the business and create interest
- **Every slide reduces uncertainty** for the audience
- **Replace vague language** with specific, labeled data
- **Visual consistency** keeps focus on content
- **Personalization** (investor name, tailored note) → 29% lift; deeper tailoring → up to 47% when authentic

## Investor Pitch Deck Template

Use this 10–12 slide order. Front-load substance.

1. **Mission/Purpose** — One sentence: why the company exists
2. **Problem** — Specific, quantified pain points with customer context
3. **Solution** — One clear sentence; why existing alternatives fail
4. **Market Opportunity** — Realistic sizing, evidence of how money flows
5. **Traction** — Real metrics: revenue, users, growth rates, partnerships (not claims)
6. **Product/Service Overview** — Value, not features; tie to outcomes
7. **Team** — Founder-market fit, execution readiness. Investors spend ~43% of reading time here. Include client logos if available (97% of top decks).
8. **Business Model** — How you make money
9. **Ask and Use of Funds** — How much, what it funds, next stage
10. **Next Steps / CTA** — Clear action: “Let’s talk”, “Book a meeting”. Decks with CTA book 22% more meetings.
11. **Appendix (optional)** — Case studies, testimonials, timeline, detailed metrics

### Slides present in top decks

- Client logos (97%) — proof of traction
- Team (96%) — investors spend ~43% of reading time on this slide
- About us (93%) — short, crisp framing
- Product/service overview (87%) — value, not features
- Case studies/testimonials (81%) — proof
- Implementation/timeline (63%) — go-to-market thinking
- Next steps/CTA (57%) — decks with CTA book 22% more meetings

## General Presentation

Same principles apply to sales, internal, and conference talks:

1. **Opening impact** — Hook in the first 10 seconds; substance by slide 3
2. **Front-load substance** — Lead with your strongest content (traction, proof, insight)
3. **Headline test** — Titles explain the story on their own
4. **10–15 slides** — Enough depth; no bloat
5. **Clear CTA** — What should the audience do next?
6. **Mobile-friendly** — Readable on small screens if shared async

Adapt slide structure to purpose:
- **Sales deck**: Problem → Solution → Proof → Pricing → Next step
- **Internal**: Context → Recommendation → Evidence → Ask
- **Conference**: Hook → Thesis → 3 pillars → Takeaway → CTA

## Instructions

1. **Gather Information**: Use discovery spec (pn-discovery-questionnaire), PRD (pn-create-prd), or content strategy (pn-content-strategy) when available. Ask for traction metrics, team bios, and target audience.

2. **Apply Template**: For investor decks, use the 10–12 slide order above. For other types, follow General Presentation structure.

3. **Output Format**: Produce a structured markdown outline/spec. Each slide = heading + bullet points or draft copy. Format for human or tool to turn into slides.

4. **Save Output**: Save as `docs/pitch-[product-name].md` or `docs/decks/YYYY-MM-DD-[slug].md`.

5. **Validation Checklist**:
   - [ ] Slide titles pass headline test
   - [ ] Slides 1–3 front-load substance
   - [ ] Total 10–15 slides (investor: 10–12)
   - [ ] Clear CTA on final slide
   - [ ] Specific data, no vague claims
   - [ ] Team slide included (investor decks)
   - [ ] Mobile-readable if shared async
