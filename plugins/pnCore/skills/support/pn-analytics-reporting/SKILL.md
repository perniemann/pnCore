# Analytics reporting

## When to use

- Business intelligence and KPI dashboards.
- Statistical analysis, forecasting, trends.
- Marketing attribution, ROI tracking.
- Customer segmentation, lifetime value.

## Workflow

1. **Discovery:** Data sources, quality, stakeholder needs.
2. **Analysis:** Methodology, hypotheses, statistical testing.
3. **Visualization:** Dashboards, executive summaries.
4. **Delivery:** Recommendations with implementation notes.

## Guardrails

- Report confidence levels and sample sizes.
- Document sources and assumptions.
- Reproducible workflows when possible.
