# Product and operational financial analysis

> **Scope note:** This skill covers product metrics, operational budgets, and unit economics for engineering and product teams. For professional financial analysis (DCF models, comparable company analysis, investment committee memos, GL reconciliation) use the FSI skill stack: `pn-fsi-analyst-discipline`, `pn-dcf-model`, `pn-comps-analysis`, `pn-ic-memo`, `pn-gl-reconciler`, `pn-earnings-analysis`, `pn-market-research`.

## When to use

- Product KPI tracking and dashboard design.
- Unit economics modeling (CAC, LTV, payback period).
- Operational budget planning and variance analysis.
- A/B test ROI evaluation and cost/benefit framing.
- Engineering cost estimates and cloud spend analysis.

## Workflow

1. **Define metrics:** Identify KPIs, dimensions, and reporting period.
2. **Validate data:** Reconcile sources; establish baselines and prior-period comparators.
3. **Analyze:** Variance analysis, trend identification, scenario modeling.
4. **Report:** Executive summary with action items; flag assumptions clearly.

## Guardrails

- Validate all data sources; document every assumption.
- No confidential data in prompts; use aggregates or anonymized figures.
- Label estimates explicitly; do not present modeled numbers as actuals.
- For investment-grade or compliance-adjacent analysis, escalate to the FSI skill stack.
